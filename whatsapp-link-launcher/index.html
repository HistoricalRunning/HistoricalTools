WhatsApp Link Launcher

Open chats, create shareable links and join groups using an invite.

Include country code. Formatting characters are allowed; omit any local trunk zero after the country code.
Requires a valid group invite URL or invite code, provided by a group member or admin. A group name or ID cannot grant access.
No server, analytics, or storage. Numbers stay in your browser until you open or share the generated WhatsApp URL.
